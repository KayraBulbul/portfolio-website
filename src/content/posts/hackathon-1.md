## Our Project

So our project is apart of the "Accessible Education" track. It's a browser extension mixed with a dashboard webapp and some cool AI integration. The extension itself allows the user to apply filters/transformations to a webpage to make it easier for them to read. We've included a bunch of customisable transformations that can be applied to hopefully help a broad range of users with different impairments.

The dashboard is a place where users can save webpages with their selected transformations, allowing them to quickly come back and not have to redo their settings. You can also do things like group pages together into folders, favourite pages for quicker access to higher priority or frequently accessed pages and also use our AI integration tool to help with things like summarising, simplifying or turning images to text.

I think this project is coming out really well so far, I was mainly working on the backend API/database stuff and I believe all has been implemented so far. Just waiting on the actual extension and dashboard frontend to finish up before we can begin working on our presentation. I'm really hoping we end up getting picked as finalists, that'd be so cool especially since it's everyone's first hackathon, but I'm fine with whatever placement we get. I've really enjoyed sitting and working with like-minded people and hope to keep in contact with my teammates after this hackathon is over.

We have to submit everything by Monday night so in two days and we'll know if we're a finalist team sometime during the week, in which case we're going to have to prepare a pitch/presentation in person during the closing ceremony on Friday.
